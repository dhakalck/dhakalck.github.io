At a glance

Full details are in the downloadable documents below. Highlights:

Current Position Health Economist, CDC Foundation (contract to CDC), Atlanta, GA — Dec 2021 to Present. Data to Policy Lead, Bloomberg Philanthropies Data for Health Initiative, supporting the Division of Global Health Protection across 19 countries.
Education PhD, Applied Economics, University of Georgia (2022) · MS, Statistics, University of Georgia (2021) · MS, Applied Economics, Texas Tech University (2016)
Research 19 peer-reviewed publications in JAMA Network Open, Journal of Policy Analysis and Management, American Journal of Preventive Medicine, Diabetes, Obesity & Metabolism, and others; 7 working papers and manuscripts under review or revision. See Publications.
Methods Causal inference and quasi-experimental methods (DiD, synthetic control, RD, IV); structural econometric modeling; health economic evaluation (CEA, CUA, BIA); decision-analytic modeling (Markov, microsimulation); machine learning. Tools: R, Stata, SAS, Python, SQL, AWS, Tableau, Power BI.
Honors 2020 Outstanding Article Award, Journal of Agricultural and Applied Economics · Early Career Research Scholar, USAID Feed the Future Innovation Lab (2014) · International Graduate Student Fellowship, USAID Feed the Future Innovation Lab (2011)
Service Peer reviewer for JAMA Network Open, Health Services Research, BMJ, American Journal of Preventive Medicine, Applied Economics, PLOS ONE, Nutrients, and others. Member: ASHEcon, AAEA, Southern Economics Association.
Prior Roles Graduate Research & Teaching Assistant, University of Georgia (2016–2022) · Assistant Professor, Tribhuvan University, Nepal (2014–2015) · Research Associate / Data Analyst, Nepal Development Research Institute (2012–2013)
